# Nature / NHB navigation evidence: supplied bibliography and scope audit

## Verification and interpretation boundary

All eight entries below were supplied by the project owner as verified citations. In this task, direct requests to Nature, DOI.org and Crossref returned **403 Forbidden** from the environment proxy. No abstract or full text was independently accessed. Consequently the machine-readable registry records `metadata: project-owner-supplied` and `content: not-independently-accessed`; source-linked concepts are **provisional conceptual mappings**, not independently verified direct support for an operationalization.

Journal/year/volume/pages/DOIs are transcribed from the supplied request. S8 has no supplied DOI and remains DOI-unresolved. No sample size, detailed method, effect size or coefficient is invented. Study contexts below are limited to titles and supplied scope. Bibliographic-format tests are not literature verification.

Publication class (`nature-review` / `nature-primary`) describes the supplied publication, not the validity of any platform predictor. Source verification, conceptual relevance, factor operationalization, implementation and applicability are separate fields. No source validates this HME architecture or the Showcase.

## S1 — Yavuz & Spiers (2026)

Yavuz, E. & Spiers, H. J. Determinants of individual navigation ability. *Nature Reviews Psychology* **5**, 273–289 (2026).

- **DOI:** [10.1038/s44159-026-00544-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-026-00544-3)
- **H/E/M/task mapping:** Human; historical Environment; technology-exposure role of Machine.
- **Study context:** Review of navigation determinants, according to supplied citation/scope.
- **Supplied conceptual support:** Distal/lifetime physical, cultural and social exposure; ageing; sleep/exercise; current exposure; technology use; personal attributes.
- **Does not establish here:** No specific genetics coefficient, technology effect direction, deterministic lifetime pathway, validated SoD scale, anxiety predictor or intervention policy follows from this conceptual scope.
- **Architecture implication:** Separate distal determinants, lifestyle/current exposure, personal capability, and navigation traits; keep technology exposure distinct from active intervention.

## Before future scientific implementation

Human scientific review should inspect the actual paper, confirm the bibliography (including S8 DOI), delimit the relevant passage/task/population, select an operationalization and scale, and version an explicit model binding. A review may organize determinants without supporting a coefficient. A primary study may support an association or effect in its setting without establishing transportability to hospitals, older adults, walkers, wheelchair users or drivers. Registering a source/model does not establish causality.

This refinement contains no SoD/anxiety equation, lostness probability/CNN-BiLSTM, entropy/complexity/salience algorithm, grid-cell model, GPS effect, adaptive cue policy or smartwatch intervention. The Nature/NHB map is static; it performs no runtime web requests and does not control the Showcase.
